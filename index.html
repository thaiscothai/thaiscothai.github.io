Meet Chef Pirin

From Thailand to Scotland, and back to Thailand. Chef Pirin Prathepha Greig spent two decades in professional kitchens in Scotland before returning to her homeland.

Her cooking brings together authentic Thai flavours, years of experience and a passion for sharing dishes you can make at home.

Discover more of her journey in the cookbook.
